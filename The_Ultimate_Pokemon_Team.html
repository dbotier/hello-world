ULTIMATE KANTO TEAM

You know kiddo, after a lifetime of studying Pokemon, trainers all over the world would come to me and brag about their Poke-God squad. Claims of an unbeatable team were almost as common as my grandson forgetting to call me each day. Haha!

Building the ultimate team forces a strong willed trainer to set aside his favoritism and biases. Synergy, speed, power, and matchups are essential and can make or break your team!

I have taken a look at the Pokemon home to Kanto, the original region of the Pokemon world, and have put together some special research! I analyzed the elite 4 of Kanto, consisting of Lorelei the Ice type trainer, Bruno the Fighting type trainer, Agatha the Poison type trainer, Lance the Dragon type trainer, and the formidable champion with a well rounded gauntlet of Pokemon.

Here is what I consider the most formidable 6 Pokemon team across the Kanto region.

Alakazam is the key to the greatest Kanto team. Its psychic typing is quite overpowered in Kanto. The Pokemon type chart demonstrates dark types as a counter to psychics, but dark types were only discovered in generation 2, after the Kanto elite 4 had established itself! Haha! Alakazam and his spoons sweep past most of Bruno’s fighting Pokemon. Additionally, Agatha’s ghost Pokemon are all poison types as well, so Alakazam's psychic super-effectiveness paired with its strong 120 speed stat and 500 base (combined) stat total solidify its place in the team

Arcanine is a great partner to have for any serious trainer. Extremely high base stats of 555, almost as high as the amount of times my grandson forgot to call me! A legend amongst the Pokemon present in Kanto, the fire type Arcanine finds its place in any triumphant team.

Jolteon, the electric type, can blast out 10000-volt lightning bolts. WOWZA! Now pair that with the speed (130) to strike first against nearly all Kanto Pokemon and we have a winner.

Lapras, the dual water-ice type, is a nearly-extinct Pokemon but a gentle soul that can read the minds of people. A great counter for Lance’s dragon types as it deals super effective blows. Lapras is beloved as it ferries Kanto residents across waters, and would also be beloved by a Pokemon champion!

Snorlax usually spends his days by eating, sleeping, and being lazy. However, this absolute tank of a Pokemon is unbeatable on his day. Off the charts levels of health (160HP along with 540 base stats), can sling some crazy damage, and is the best known counter for Alakazam. The normal type of legend.

Last but certainly not least, we have Dragonite! An extremely rare Pokemon more famously known through Pokemon folk-lore as the mysterious, lonely sea guardian, but a noble trainer’s quality allowed us to research this pseudo-legendary Pokemon! With the highest base stat total (600) found in Kanto, along with its striking 134 attack, this dragon-flying sprints to the top of the ultimate Pokemon team! Consider it the ace of the team.
